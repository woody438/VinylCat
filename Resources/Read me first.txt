VINYLCAT — INSTALLING

You need macOS 15 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.


READ THIS FIRST: THE APP IS NOT SIGNED BY APPLE

VinylCat is ad-hoc signed on the machine that built it. It is NOT signed with an
Apple Developer ID and it is NOT notarised by Apple.

macOS WILL REFUSE TO OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME. This is expected. The download is
not broken and there is nothing wrong with the app — it is Gatekeeper doing its
job on an unnotarised app. Follow steps 1-6 and you are in.

Note: right-click -> Open does NOT get around this on macOS 15 or later. That
old workaround is gone. System Settings is the only way in.


1. Drag VinylCat onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Applications and double-click VinylCat. macOS says
   "Apple could not verify VinylCat is free of malware", with only a Done
   button. Click Done.
   Nothing has gone wrong. This step is REQUIRED — it is what tells macOS that
   an app was blocked, which puts the button you need into System Settings.

3. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security.

4. Scroll ALL THE WAY to the bottom, to the Security section. You will see
   "VinylCat was blocked to protect your Mac." Click OPEN ANYWAY next to it.
   That line only appears after step 2. If it isn't there, go back and
   double-click the app again.

5. Authenticate with Touch ID or your password if asked, then click OPEN ANYWAY
   again in the confirmation dialog.

6. VinylCat opens. YOU ONLY DO THIS ONCE. Every launch after this is a normal
   double-click.


THEN: FIRST-RUN SETUP

7. The setup wizard asks for Camera, Microphone and Speech Recognition and
   checks that Dictation is switched on. Click ALLOW on each. Once only.

8. Enter your own Discogs token and your OpenAI or Gemini key in the wizard.
   The wizard says where to get them. Identifying a record costs about a tenth
   of a cent, so the model account needs billing set up.

   Adding your matches to your Discogs Collection is OFF until you switch it on
   — either in the wizard's Discogs step or later in Settings. Until you do,
   VinylCat neither writes to nor reads from your Discogs Collection.


UPDATING

Drag the new VinylCat over the old one in Applications and replace it. Your
catalogue and photos are kept in your own Library folder and are not touched.


WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES

~/Library/Application Support/VinylCat/
