VINYLCAT — INSTALLING

You need macOS 15 or later on an Apple silicon Mac.

1. Drag VinylCat onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Applications and double-click VinylCat. macOS will say
   "Apple could not verify VinylCat is free of malware". Click Done.
   (This happens because the app is not notarised with Apple. Right-click → Open
   does not get around it on macOS 15.)

3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to the bottom and click
   Open Anyway, then Open Anyway again in the confirmation.

4. VinylCat launches. The setup wizard asks for Camera, Microphone and Speech
   Recognition and checks that Dictation is switched on. Click ALLOW on each.
   You only do this once.

5. Enter your own Discogs token and your OpenAI or Gemini key in the wizard.
   The wizard says where to get them. Identifying a record costs about a tenth
   of a cent, so the model account needs billing set up.

UPDATING

Drag the new VinylCat over the old one in Applications and replace it. Your
catalogue and photos are kept in your own Library folder and are not touched.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
